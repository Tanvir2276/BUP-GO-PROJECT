Simple 3-Step Process

How BUPGO Works

Connecting students traveling on the same route for safer, faster university transit.

🔍

1. Search or Post a Ride

Heading to campus from Mirpur, Uttara, or Dhanmondi? Search existing rides or post your vehicle/CNG share plan with departure time.

🤝

2. Connect & Confirm

Passengers send a seat request. The student driver reviews the applicant's BUP ID and department before confirming the seat.

🚀

3. Travel Together & Save

Meet at the pickup point, commute to BUP comfortably, split fuel/transportation fares, and make your trip stress-free.
